What do we do?

We Put Your Pitch on a Gift Keepsake--to Help You Win the Heart and the Mind.

Give Muse a pitch — or a whole PPT deck — and p2gift turns it into one printed gift: pick the pitch, pick the gift, pick a layout, approve the artwork, check out. Every pick is a tap in the chat.

How to start?

One tap copies the connect message. Paste it to Muse, and the dialog starts.

Copies: "Custom connect to p2gift: https://p2gift.keeg.uk/mcp. Connect, save it as a reusable skill."
